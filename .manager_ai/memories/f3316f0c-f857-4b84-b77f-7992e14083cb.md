Specs produced by SpecWriter consistently lack explicit "Acceptance Criteria" and "Non-Goals" sections. Functional requirements and technical constraints are well-covered, but there's no clear "you know it's done when" checklist or explicit out-of-scope boundaries. This creates minor ambiguity for PlanWriter and Developer downstream. Should be a review checklist item for SpecReviewer to catch and add.
