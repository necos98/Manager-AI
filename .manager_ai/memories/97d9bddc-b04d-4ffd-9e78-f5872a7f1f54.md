When reject_step() or finished_pipeline_step() signals _execute() via set_step_completed(), the signal must happen AFTER session.commit(). Otherwise _execute() session refreshes from DB, sees stale pre-commit data, and can overwrite REJECTED with COMPLETED. Fix pattern: commit first, signal second. Applies to all pipeline step completion signaling paths.
