get_run(), get_runs_for_issue(), and get_active_runs_for_project() all manually construct ~40 lines of nearly identical dicts from PipelineRun ORM objects. The step-run mapping block is duplicated verbatim. If PipelineRunResponse or PipelineStepRunResponse schemas change, all three must be updated. Future refactor should extract a shared _run_to_dict() or _step_run_to_dict() helper method.
