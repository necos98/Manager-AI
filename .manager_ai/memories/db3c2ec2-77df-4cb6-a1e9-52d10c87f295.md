Plans often assume FastAPI exception types (RequestValidationError, AppError) are auto-logged to root logger at ERROR level. They are NOT. FastAPI handles them via @app.exception_handler with no logging. Plans must include explicit ErrorLoggerService.log_exception() calls in exception handlers for RequestValidationError and AppError. Also check asyncio exception handler chaining — lifespan may already have a handler, new one must wrap not replace.
