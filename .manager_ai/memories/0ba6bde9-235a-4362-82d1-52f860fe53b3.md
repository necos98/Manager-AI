When extracting functions between modules, the logger dependency is commonly missed. `_load_project_into_memory` in main.py used `logger.warning/info` from the module-level `logger = logging.getLogger(__name__)` — this breaks when moved to a new module without adding `import logging` and `logger = logging.getLogger(__name__)`. Always audit for implicit module-level references (logger, constants, config, globals) when refactoring across files.
