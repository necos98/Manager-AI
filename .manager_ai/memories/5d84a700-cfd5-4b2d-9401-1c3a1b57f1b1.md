In the project_loader.py extraction, _FRONTMATTER_RE was promoted from per-call recompile (inside the function body) to module-level (compiled once at import). _parse_fm also promoted to module-level. Nested closures _opt_str, _as_str, _link_from_dict were also promoted to module-level. The redundant `import re as _re` was left inside the function body rather than auditing which local imports could be removed — the spec explicitly chose consistency over cleanup to minimize risk of missing an indirect usage.
