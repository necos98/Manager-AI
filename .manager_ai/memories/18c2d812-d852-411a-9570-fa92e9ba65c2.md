EventService (app/services/event_service.py) exposes emit(event: dict) to broadcast events to all registered notifiers. The notify() method exists only on BaseNotifier subclasses (WebSocketNotifier, NotificationService) — NOT on EventService itself. The issues router (app/routers/issues.py) had two bugs where event_service.notify() was called instead of event_service.emit(), causing AttributeError on both complete_issue and force_finish_issue endpoints.
