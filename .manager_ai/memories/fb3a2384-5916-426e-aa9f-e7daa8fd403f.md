The health endpoint resource_consistency check now uses a ReadCache with 5-min TTL to avoid rescanning all YAML files on every 30s poll. Cache is invalidated by watcher (via clear_all_caches) and by install_manager_json (explicit clear). Cache key: f"health:{project.id}". The scan logic itself (_check_resource_consistency) is unchanged.
