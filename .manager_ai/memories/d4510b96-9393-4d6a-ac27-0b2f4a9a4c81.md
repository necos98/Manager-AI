IssueQueueService.startup_resume() scans all projects for pending QueueEntries at application startup and auto-starts the first queued issue if nothing is running. Called from main.py as a fire-and-forget task after IssueQueueService registration. This makes the queue resilient to restarts — previously the queue was purely event-driven and would stall after restart until a new issue was manually added. Added in issue b6b04991-312b-499b-9fdf-468bdd3a0431.
