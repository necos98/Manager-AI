Module-level dict `_issue_completion_locks` in `issue_service.py` stores per-issue asyncio.Lock for race guard in `complete_issue()`. Must ALWAYS `.pop(issue_id, None)` inside the `async with lock:` block before `return rec`. Without pop, dict grows unbounded. Only `complete_issue()` uses this dict — `cancel_issue()` and `force_finish_issue()` intentionally bypass locks.
