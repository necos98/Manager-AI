_terminal_reader() in terminal_session.py previously accessed service._lock, service._terminals, and service._queues directly — a coupling smell that broke when Lock became async. Fix: use public service.get() with try/except KeyError, and the new async get_log_queue() helper. Future changes must never access TerminalService private members from outside the class.
