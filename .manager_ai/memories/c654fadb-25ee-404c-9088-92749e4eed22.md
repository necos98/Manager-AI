Pipeline step rejection creates duplicate PipelineStepRun rows. When querying the latest step_run, use `scalars().first()` with `ORDER BY started_at DESC NULLS LAST` — NOT `scalar_one_or_none()` (raises MultipleResultsFound). Also: `set_step_completed()` must fire AFTER `session.commit()` — otherwise `_execute()`'s `session.refresh()` sees stale pre-rejection state and incorrectly marks the step COMPLETED instead of detecting REJECTED.
