If run_issue() fails after QueueEntry→DISPATCHING but issue stays REASONING, queue was permanently blocked. Fixed: _count_active_reasoning() only counts REASONING issues with active QueueEntry (PENDING/DISPATCHING). Failed/dispatched QueueEntries = ghost, ignored. Applied in _maybe_auto_start_first and startup_resume.
