CRITICAL BUG PATTERN: When converting `_execute()` from `for i, step in enumerate(steps)` to `while run.current_step_index < len(steps)`, the `run.current_step_index += 1` for normal step completion was omitted. Any future conversion of indexed loops to while-loop pattern must verify the increment variable advances on all paths (success, rejection, failure). Fix: add `run.current_step_index += 1` after step_run.status = COMPLETED (around line 325 in pipeline_run_service.py).
