reject_step() always creates a brand new PipelineStepRun for the target step with RUNNING status. Old step_runs stay REJECTED as permanent audit trail. No step_run is ever reused or deleted. This means _execute() must fetch the LATEST step_run by created_at DESC when resolving a step, since multiple may exist for the same pipeline_step_id.
