All cross-project operations that iterate over project directories must call `ProjectService.list_all(archived=False)`, never `archived=None`. Archived projects may point to unreachable network paths — iterating over them causes hangs during cache/index rebuilds and MCP tool calls.

Affected locations (as of 2026-05-15):
- `issue_service.py` — `get_by_id()`
- `memory_service.py` — `_locate_memory()`
- `task_service.py` — `_project_path_for_issue()`, `get_by_id()`, `update()`, `delete()`
- `issue_relation_service.py` — `_all_paths()`
- `mcp/server.py` — `update_task_status()`, `update_task_name()`, `delete_task()`
- `routers/projects.py` — rebuild-index endpoint has explicit guard against archived projects

When adding new cross-project scans, always use `archived=False` unless there is a specific reason to include archived projects.
