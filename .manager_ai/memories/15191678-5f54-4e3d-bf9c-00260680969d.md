_maybe_auto_start_first() now uses `if pending_count < 1: return` instead of the old `if pending_count != 1: return`. This means it auto-starts whenever there's at least one pending QueueEntry and no issue is currently REASONING for the project. The old guard was too conservative — it blocked auto-start when the queue had >1 pending (e.g., auto-processing enabled after multiple issues queued, or startup_resume() failure). The running-check guard and per-project lock in _dequeue_and_run() prevent double-start. Single-file change in issue_queue_service.py line 538. Test updated: test_skips_when_multiple_pending → test_auto_starts_when_multiple_pending.
