NewIssueDialog (new-issue-dialog.tsx) has abandon-confirmation when closing dirty form. Intercepts onOpenChange(false) when isDirty (description, priority, tags, category changed from defaults). Shows inline confirmation inside same DialogContent — no AlertDialog needed. Confirmation has "No, keep editing" (outline, goes back) and "Yes, abandon" (destructive, resets + closes). X button hidden during confirm via showCloseButton prop. Escape during confirm = abandon.
