The "Create issue from here" feature adds source_issue_id to IssueCreate schema. Relation creation (related) happens at the router level after IssueService.create(), not inside IssueService. This follows the existing pattern (e.g. complete_issue fires hooks in router after service call). IssueRelationService.add_relation() normalizes RELATED type by sorting IDs alphabetically. No relation cache invalidation needed — invalidate_issue_cache() is a documented no-op in the RAM-first architecture.
